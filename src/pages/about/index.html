About Pure Divine Energy

Healing with compassion, stillness and a deeper connection to divine energy.

Pure Divine Energy is a sacred healing and learning space guided by Jyotsana Jain, Reiki Grandmaster and spiritual healer, devoted to helping seekers restore peace, balance and clarity in body, mind and soul.

Hi, I’m Jyotsana Jain

For years, I have supported individuals through Reiki healing, distance healing, chakra balancing, spiritual counselling and authentic energy practices. My approach is gentle, grounded and heart-led, so each person feels safe, seen and supported throughout their healing journey.

I believe healing is not just about removing pain. It is about helping you return to your natural inner balance, soften emotional heaviness, and reconnect with the calm wisdom already present within you.

Authentic Reiki Grandmaster guidance Deep spiritual practice rooted in traditional energy healing and real experience.
Distance and in-person support Healing and guidance designed to be accessible, personal and practical.
Courses with clarity and care Structured learning for seekers who wish to understand, practice and grow deeply.

What this space stands for

Pure Divine Energy is built on calm, trust and transformation. Everything here is designed to help you move from overwhelm to alignment in a graceful and spiritually nourishing way.

  • Healing that feels safe and heart-centred
  • Spiritual learning without confusion or clutter
  • Guidance for body, mind and soul together

Who this helps

This work supports people facing emotional heaviness, inner restlessness, energy imbalance, spiritual confusion, chronic stress, or a sincere desire to learn Reiki and healing practices.

Our intention

To create a sacred yet professional healing environment where seekers can receive support, learn authentic practices and reconnect with their own divine light.
