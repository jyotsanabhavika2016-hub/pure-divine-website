How do I know which healing service is right for me?

You can share your current concern through the contact page or WhatsApp. Based on your situation, Pure Divine Energy can guide you toward a suitable session.

Are distance healing sessions available?

Distance and online support can be offered depending on the service and your individual requirement.

Can beginners receive healing support?

Yes. The services are designed to be approachable for beginners as well as people already on a healing or spiritual path.

What kinds of support are available?

The published services include Reiki healing, 1-on-1 guidance, chakra balancing and aura cleansing.

What happens after I send an enquiry?

After receiving your query, the team will contact you shortly to understand the support you are looking for.

Can I visit the centre in Jaipur?

Pure Divine Energy is based behind Hotel Teej, Bani Park, Jaipur. You can contact the team before visiting or use the map on the contact page.
